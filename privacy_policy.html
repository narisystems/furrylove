Privacy Policy

Your pet's health records stay on your phone. This page explains exactly what that means, and the little that is sent.

App: Pet Health Records: Furry Love (com.narisystems.furrylove)  ·  Developer: NaRiSystems  ·  Last updated: October 2026

In short

Furry Love has no account, no ads and no cloud. Your pets' names, records, photos and documents are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded to us or to anyone.
  • We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and the app never reads your GPS location.
  • We do not show ads and we do not sell or share data for advertising.
  • The app sends two kinds of anonymous technical data to help us fix problems and understand which features are used: crash reports and simple usage counts. They never contain your pets' names, records, notes, photos or documents.
  • Records leave your phone only when you choose to share a backup or an export.

What is stored on your phone

Everything you enter is kept in the app's private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it:

  • Pet profiles (name, species, breed, birth date, sex, microchip number, your vet's contact, photo).
  • Vaccines and preventives, medications and dose history, vet visits and costs, weights, and any custom logs you create.
  • Documents and photos you add (scans, pictures and PDFs).
  • Your settings, and whether you have unlocked the app.

The app is set so that Android does not copy this data to Google's automatic backups. When you uninstall the app, Android removes it. A backup file you made yourself is not removed, because it is your file.

What the app sends, and to whom

PurposeServiceWhat is sent
Crash reports Sentry When the app crashes: the technical error trace, the app version, the Android version and the phone model. The report also carries a random identifier that the app creates when it is installed (it is not your name, email, phone number, Google account or advertising ID, and we cannot tell who you are from it), and Sentry works out an approximate location, such as the city or country, from the network address when the report arrives. We have switched off storing the address itself. No pet or record data, no screenshots, and no performance tracing.
Anonymous usage counts Aptabase Short event names such as "app opened", "pet added", "vaccine added", "backup completed", "export created", and which help page was opened or which free limit was reached. The few details sent are fixed words (for example pdf or csv), never anything you typed. No account, no advertising ID. The service may work out a rough location, such as the country, from the network address when an event arrives.
Buying and restoring Unlock Google Play Google handles the payment and tells the app whether your Google account owns Unlock. We never see your payment details.

These services act on our behalf to run the app and are bound by their own privacy policies, linked above. We use the data only to find and fix problems and to decide which features to improve. We do not sell it and we do not share it for advertising.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to send your reminders.
  • Internet: for Google Play purchases, crash reports, usage counts and opening links.
  • Run at startup: so reminders are planned again after your phone restarts.

The app does not ask for your camera, photos, files, location, contacts or microphone. Scanning a document uses Google's document scanner, which runs on your phone, and choosing a photo or file uses Android's own picker; the app receives only the picture or file you choose.

Backups and exports

"Back up now" creates one file on your phone and opens the Android share sheet. "Export records" (part of Unlock) creates a PDF or a spreadsheet the same way. Where that file goes is up to you and the app you choose (Google Drive, email, a chat app). Once you send it, it is in that service's hands and under its policy. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

Your choices and your data

  • You can view, edit and delete any record in the app. Deleting a pet deletes its records, photos and documents.
  • Because we hold no account and no copy of your records, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Uninstalling the app removes the data from your phone.
  • Crash reports and usage counts are anonymous and cannot be traced back to you, so they cannot be looked up or deleted individually. If you would like to ask about them, write to us.

Not medical advice

Furry Love is a record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment or judge whether a value is normal. For any question about your pet's health, ask your veterinarian.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes (for example a new service is added), we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.
